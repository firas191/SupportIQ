# Orders and returns (English)

## Changing an order after checkout

An order can be modified as long as it has not been handed to the carrier — usually within two
hours of checkout. After that point the warehouse has already picked the items and the order can
only be returned once delivered.

Address changes are the one exception: they can be requested until the parcel leaves the sorting
centre, through "My orders" then "Edit delivery address".

## Cancelling an order

Cancellation is free and immediate while the order status is "Preparing". Once the status turns to
"Shipped", the order must be received and then returned using the prepaid label.

Refunds for cancelled orders are issued within 3 working days, back to the original payment method.

## Return conditions

Items must be returned unused, in their original packaging, within 14 calendar days of delivery.
Personalised items and activated gift cards cannot be returned.

A return is free when it uses the prepaid label provided. Returns sent by other means are at the
customer's expense and are not reimbursed.

## Warranty

All products carry a two-year legal warranty against manufacturing defects. Wear and tear,
accidental damage and misuse are excluded.

A warranty claim requires the order number and photographs of the defect. Repair or replacement is
decided after technical assessment, within 10 working days of receiving the item.
